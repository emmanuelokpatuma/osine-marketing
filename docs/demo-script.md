# Demo script for Global Opportunity Radar

## Goal

Show that the product is a serious public-source intelligence platform for business opportunity discovery, not a generic OSINT demo.

## 1. Opening (10–15 seconds)

"This is a public-source opportunity intelligence platform designed to help sales and growth teams find high-fit business opportunities from free and public data sources before competitors do."

## 2. Show the homepage (15–20 seconds)

Point to the hero section and explain:

- it monitors public activity across target regions
- it surfaces high-value opportunity signals
- it ranks them by relevance, urgency, and fit

## 3. Explain the value proposition (20–30 seconds)

"The problem is not a lack of market information. The problem is fragmented signals spread across government portals, company registries, news feeds, hiring data, and local public sources. We turn that noise into a prioritized opportunity pipeline."

## 4. Walk through the live opportunity feed (30–45 seconds)

Select one or two opportunities and explain:

- why the company or sector is relevant
- which signal triggered the opportunity
- what evidence supports the ranking
- what action a sales or growth team could take next

Example framing:

"This company shows public hiring growth, expansion news, and sector activity. Combined, those signals indicate likely demand and a strong fit for outreach."

## 5. Show the source coverage (15–20 seconds)

Explain that the product is built around broad but structured coverage:

- public procurement and tenders
- company registries and filings
- hiring signals
- planning and local development activity
- public news and press coverage
- regional and sector intelligence

## 6. Show the configuration flow (15–20 seconds)

Open the settings page and explain:

- target regions can be selected
- industries and profiles can be defined
- the platform focuses the monitoring on relevant markets

## 7. Close with business outcome (10–15 seconds)

"This is not just monitoring public data. It converts public signals into an actionable opportunity stream for lead generation, sales outreach, and market intelligence."

## Suggested closing line

"We help teams discover better opportunities earlier, using public data, with evidence and prioritization built in."
