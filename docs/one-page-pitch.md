# Global Opportunity Radar

## One-page pitch

Global Opportunity Radar is a public-source intelligence platform for sales, growth, and business development teams. It continuously scans public data across target regions and industries to surface high-value opportunities before they become obvious.

The platform monitors a wide range of public signals, including government procurement activity, company expansion, hiring surges, news-driven momentum, local planning activity, and sector-level developments. Each signal is ranked by relevance, urgency, and commercial potential, then presented as a prioritized opportunity stream.

This is not a generic OSINT demo. It is a business intelligence and lead-generation engine built to find buying intent in open data.

### The problem

Businesses waste time on manual research, stale lead lists, and fragmented market signals. Public data is available everywhere, but it is scattered across governments, registries, news feeds, and local portals. The result is missed opportunities and slower pipeline development.

### The solution

Global Opportunity Radar consolidates public-source signals into a single workflow:

- monitor the public web, government portals, company registries, and industry feeds
- detect relevant opportunities and growth signals in target regions
- score and rank signal quality based on fit, urgency, and business value
- deliver a prioritized stream of opportunities into a live dashboard

### Who it helps

- B2B sales teams
- Growth teams and agencies
- Business development teams
- Recruitment agencies
- Procurement and market intelligence teams
- Consultants and exporters

### Why it matters

The most valuable pipeline is often hidden in public activity that is not yet being acted on. Signals such as new contracts, hiring surges, expansion plans, and local development efforts can reveal strong buying intent early. This product turns that signal into commercial action.

### Product value

- Discover better opportunities earlier
- Reduce time spent on manual research
- Prioritize leads using evidence and scoring
- Monitor multiple regions and sectors at once
- Build a better pipeline from public data

### Positioning

Global Opportunity Radar is positioned as a public-source opportunity intelligence platform, not a general-purpose surveillance product. The commercial message is clear: it helps teams find more relevant opportunities faster and with more context.

### Revenue model

- Individual / founder tier
- Team usage for sales and growth teams
- Multi-region monitoring
- Premium source coverage and custom verticals
- Enterprise reporting and integration add-ons

### Current state

The current version is a free-first prototype built with open data sources, local SQLite storage, and a regional source catalog. It is designed to evolve into a commercial product with additional integrations, alerting, and workflow automation.
