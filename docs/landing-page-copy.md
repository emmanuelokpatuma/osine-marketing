## The problem

Most teams still rely on stale lead lists, manual research, and scattered signals from multiple sources.

Public data is everywhere, but it is fragmented and hard to act on. Competition grows faster when teams miss signals that show buying intent before the market catches up.

---

## The solution

Global Opportunity Radar brings public-source intelligence into one workflow.

We continuously monitor government portals, company registries, recruiting activity, market news, and public sector data to detect companies and opportunities showing real commercial momentum.

Every signal is scored and ranked by:

- relevance to your target market
- urgency of the signal
- commercial value of the opportunity
- strength of evidence behind the result

---

## Why it matters to sales teams

Your sales pipeline is only as strong as your ability to find high-fit opportunities early.

Public signals often reveal buying intent before it becomes obvious. A company hiring aggressively, launching new projects, receiving investment, or publishing sector-related activity may be a strong opportunity for outreach.

This platform helps teams move faster and prioritize outreach with better context.

## How it works

1. Choose your target region and sector
2. Define the companies, industries, or profiles you want to track
3. Monitor public-source signals across relevant feeds
4. Get a ranked list of opportunities with evidence and reasoning
5. Prioritize outreach based on signal strength and urgency
