# PRODUCT.md

## Product Name
Global Opportunity Radar

## Core Transformation
Public data -> signals -> qualified leads -> action

## Primary User Outcome
Answer one weekly question with evidence:
"Which companies should I contact this week, and why?"

## Primary Users
- UK and global B2B consultancies
- Procurement-focused suppliers
- Recruiters and staffing teams
- Energy, retail, real-estate, and technology sales teams
- Founders seeking investor and grant opportunities

## Product Promise
The product interprets public evidence into actionable opportunities. It does not dump raw links or unqualified records.

## What Good Looks Like
- Every opportunity includes source evidence and timestamped reasoning
- Every score is explainable with transparent breakdown points
- Users can run broad discovery (all-seeing-eye mode) or priority lens mode
- Leads are exportable and compliant by default
- AI output is assistive, never presented as certainty

## ICP Inputs (must drive scoring)
- Industry focus
- Geography
- Company size band
- Minimum opportunity value
- Services/products sold
- Signal types and recency window

## Signal Principles
- Prefer business-level intelligence over person-level tracking
- Use permitted public sources and API terms
- Confidence rises when multiple independent sources align
- Show "possible requirement" language, not hard claims

## Opportunity Lifecycle
1. Public event
2. Signal detected
3. Opportunity created
4. Lead qualified
5. Outreach generated
6. CRM handoff
7. Outcome tracked

## Priority 7 Sectors (lens, not hard limit)
- Retail and grocery
- Energy and utilities
- Real estate
- Technology
- Healthcare
- Financial services
- Manufacturing and logistics

## North-Star Metrics
- Opportunities reviewed per week
- Leads exported per week
- Outreach generated per week
- Meetings booked from platform leads
- Win value attributable to platform signals
